# The Seventh Day (Remastered) - as sung
In the beginning
Before anything

Day one He spoke, and the dark gave way
And the night bowed down, to His say
Day two He stretched, the heavens wide
Day three the land rose up, from the tide

Day four He hung, the sun and the moon
And the stars came out like a choir tune
Day five He filled, the sea and the sky
Called it good, with a gleam in His eye

Day six He knelt down, low in the dust
And breathed His life, into all of us

Seven days, seven days
Seventh day, seventh day
Every star still sings His praise
Every wave, every wave
Every breath, every breath
He made it all, then He took His rest

On the seventh day, He blessed it
On the seventh day, He rested
